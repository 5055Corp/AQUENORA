Seller agreement

The rules for selling on AQUENORA.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This agreement is between you (the seller) and AQUENORA Ltd, RC 10023798, a company registered in Nigeria (“AQUENORA”, “we”, “us”). It applies when you open a store on AQUENORA, on the web or in the app. By creating a store you agree to it. Our privacy policy explains how we handle personal data.

1. What AQUENORA is

AQUENORA gives you an online store, a place in AQUENORA search, and checkout through Paystack. You are the seller of your products: the sale is between you and your buyer. AQUENORA is not the seller, maker or owner of the goods.

2. Who can sell

  • You must be 18 or over and give true details about yourself and your business.
  • New sellers are checked before they can take payments: registered businesses with their CAC details, others with a government ID, a selfie and their social media pages. We may ask for more information, and we may refuse or remove a store at our discretion.
  • The name on your payout bank account should match your verified identity or business.
  • Keep your login safe. You're responsible for what happens in your account.

3. Your store and products

  • Describe products honestly: real photos you own or may use, correct prices, sizes, materials and stock.
  • If an item is made to order, give a realistic time and keep to it.
  • Prices shown to buyers are what they pay for the items (plus any delivery you set).

4. Things you can't sell

You must not list or sell:

  • Counterfeit or fake branded goods, or anything that copies someone else's trademark or design
  • Weapons, ammunition, explosives or parts of them
  • Illegal drugs, prescription-only medicines, and drug equipment
  • Tobacco, vapes, and alcohol (unless you hold the licences the law requires)
  • Adult or sexual content and products
  • Stolen goods, or goods you have no right to sell
  • Live animals, or products from protected wildlife
  • Dangerous or hazardous materials
  • Financial products, investment schemes, cryptocurrency or gift-card trading
  • Anything illegal in Nigeria, or in the buyer's country when you ship abroad

We may hide products, pause payments or close stores that break these rules, and report illegal activity to the authorities.

5. Payments, fees and payouts

  • Buyers pay through Paystack. Paystack splits each payment: your share goes to your bank account and AQUENORA's commission to us. AQUENORA never holds your money.
  • AQUENORA's commission is a percentage of the item price (not delivery): currently 3% on the Free plan and 1% on Pro. Paystack's own processing fee is also taken from your share. Current fees are shown in the app.
  • Pro is a monthly subscription that renews automatically until you stop it in the app. Stopping takes effect at the end of the month you've paid for.
  • We'll tell you in advance, in the app or by email, before our fees change.
  • You're responsible for your own taxes on your sales.

6. Orders and delivery

  • An order appears in your app only once Paystack confirms the payment. Send or make items promptly, within the time you've shown.
  • Keep order statuses up to date (packed, shipped, ready for pickup, delivered). Buyers see them on their tracking page.
  • Use the delivery prices and couriers you've set, and share tracking numbers when you have them.

7. Returns, refunds and disputes

  • Set clear returns and refund rules for your store, and follow them. You must also follow Nigerian consumer law, including the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act, and the law of the buyer's country when you sell abroad.
  • If a buyer reports a problem (an item that didn't arrive, is wrong or is damaged), reply within 2 working days and try to sort it out with them.
  • If you can't agree, AQUENORA may look at the evidence from both sides and decide. If we decide the buyer should be refunded, the refund is taken from your sale through Paystack.
  • If a buyer's bank reverses a payment (a chargeback), the amount and any bank charges come from your sales.

8. Your buyers' personal data

  • Use buyers' names, phone numbers, emails and addresses only to complete and support their orders.
  • Send marketing emails only to buyers who said yes at checkout, using AQUENORA's campaign tool, which includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Don't sell or share buyers' data, and keep it safe, as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 requires.

9. Your content and our brand

You keep ownership of your photos, logo and descriptions. You allow AQUENORA to show them in your store, in AQUENORA search and in promoting AQUENORA. The AQUENORA name and logo belong to AQUENORA Ltd.

10. Pausing or closing a store

You can delete your store at any time in the app (Settings → Delete account). We may pause payments or close a store if it breaks this agreement, if we suspect fraud, or if the law requires it. Where we can, we'll tell you why and give you a chance to respond.

11. Liability

We work hard to keep AQUENORA running, but it's provided as it is, and we can't promise it will never be interrupted. As far as the law allows, AQUENORA is not liable for indirect losses or lost profits, and our total liability to you is limited to the fees you paid us in the 3 months before the claim. Nothing here limits rights you have under the law that can't be limited.

12. Changes to this agreement

We may update this agreement. We'll change the date at the top and tell you about important changes in the app or by email before they take effect. If you keep selling after that, the new version applies.

13. Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, and the courts of Nigeria will handle any dispute that we can't settle together first.
