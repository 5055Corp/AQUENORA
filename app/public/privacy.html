Privacy policy

How AQUENORA collects, uses and protects your personal data.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

AQUENORA (“AQUENORA”, “we”, “us”) is an online platform, based in Nigeria, that lets sellers create online stores and accept payments. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It covers the AQUENORA seller app (on the web and on iPhone), the AQUENORA store pages buyers visit, and our emails.

We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. If you have any question about this policy, email us at support@aquenora.com.

1. Data we collect

From sellers

  • Account details: your email address and password. We store your password only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Store details: your shop name, web address, country, currency, products, prices, photos, store design, logo, banner, pickup address and opening hours, shipping rates, and any social links you add.
  • Payout details: your bank name and account number. We pass these to Paystack to set up payouts to you.
  • Verification documents: before a new seller can take payments, we check who they are. Registered businesses give their business name, CAC number and CAC certificate. Sellers who aren't registered give the type of ID, a photo of a government ID, a selfie and their social media pages. Only the AQUENORA team can see these. They are never shown on your store or shared with buyers.
  • Pro plan billing: if you pay for Pro, Paystack gives us a secure code for your card plus its type and last 4 digits, so we can renew your plan each month. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Survey answers: if you take part in our seller interview, the answers you choose to give.

From buyers

  • Order details: your name, email, phone number and delivery address (or pickup choice), what you ordered and the amount paid.
  • Payment: payments are handled by Paystack. Card and bank details go straight to Paystack. We don't receive or store them.
  • Marketing choice: whether you agreed to receive emails from the seller.

Collected automatically

  • Basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser and device type, which our hosting and security services record to keep the service running and protect it from abuse.
  • The seller app saves your login on your device so you stay signed in. We don't use advertising trackers or sell data to advertisers.

2. How we use it

  • To run your store and process orders, payments, payouts and deliveries.
  • To bill the Pro plan, if you choose it.
  • To send emails about your account and orders, and campaign emails from sellers to buyers who agreed to receive them.
  • To check that sellers are real people or businesses before they can take payments (verification), and to show buyers a Verified seller badge.
  • To give support, prevent fraud and fake payments, and keep the service secure.
  • To improve AQUENORA, including from survey answers.
  • To meet our legal duties, such as tax and accounting records.

We process data because it is needed to provide the service you signed up for, because you agreed (for example to marketing emails), because the law requires it, or for our legitimate interest in running a safe service.

3. Sellers and their buyers

Each seller is responsible for how they use their buyers' details, for example to deliver orders. AQUENORA stores and processes that data on the seller's behalf. Buyers who want to correct or remove their details can contact the seller or us.

4. Who we share data with

We only share data with the companies that help us run AQUENORA, and only what they need:

  • Paystack: payments, payouts and Pro billing
  • Railway: servers and database hosting
  • Cloudflare: website delivery, security, custom domains and photo storage
  • Resend: sending emails
  • Google Fonts: the fonts on our pages

We may also share data if the law requires it, or to protect our users or AQUENORA from fraud. We never sell personal data.

5. Data stored outside Nigeria

Some of these providers store data on servers outside Nigeria, for example in the United States or Europe. When this happens we rely on the providers' security and contract commitments to keep your data protected, as the Nigeria Data Protection Act requires.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and store data: until you delete your account.
  • Verification documents: until you delete your account. If you send new documents, the old ones are deleted.
  • Order and payment records: for up to 6 years, as Nigerian tax and accounting law requires. After that they are deleted.
  • Survey answers: until they are no longer needed for research.

7. Deleting your account

Sellers can delete their account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account. Your store goes offline straight away, and your products, design, customer list, verification documents and login are deleted. Any Pro plan stops and your saved card is removed. Only the order and payment records the law requires are kept. You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing support@aquenora.com.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to: see the personal data we hold about you; correct it; delete it; stop or limit how we use it; give you a copy; or stop sending you marketing emails (every campaign email has an unsubscribe link). Email support@aquenora.com and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

9. Security

All connections use encryption (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, card details stay with Paystack, and access to data is limited to what is needed to run the service.

10. Children

AQUENORA is for sellers aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date at the top and let sellers know in the app or by email.
