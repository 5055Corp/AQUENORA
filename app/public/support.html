Common questions

How do I get paid?

Add your bank details in the app under Financials → Get paid. When a buyer pays, Paystack splits the payment automatically: your share goes straight to your bank account, usually by the next working day.

A buyer says they paid but I can't see the order

Orders only appear once Paystack confirms the payment, which protects you from fake alerts. If it still hasn't appeared after a few minutes, email orders@aquenora.com with the buyer's name and the time they paid.

How do I ship to buyers abroad?

On the Orders page, open Shipping and set your own flat rates for DHL, FedEx, UPS, Aramex, post or local delivery, by country or continent. Each international order has a printable shipment form for your courier.

How do I stop my Pro plan?

Open Plan & domain on the AQUENORA website (app.aquenora.com) and tap Stop automatic renewal. Pro stays active until the end of the month you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Home, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. You can also email us to ask. See our privacy policy for what is deleted and what the law requires us to keep.

I forgot my password

Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll help you get back into your account.
