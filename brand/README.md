# AQUENORA brand

**Mark:** a simple geometric "A" with a star of light at its peak (*illuminating possibilities*) and one thin orbit ring as its crossbar (*connecting the world*). The ring passes behind the A at the back and in front at the front, so it reads as 3D with flat shapes only. No glows or gradients.

**Colours**

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night | `#0A0612` | Dark backgrounds and the app icon; the A, star and name on light backgrounds |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | The A, star and name on dark backgrounds |
| Silver lilac | `#CFC8DA` | Orbit ring on dark |
| Lavender | `#A78BFA` | Motto on dark |
| Dusk | `#6B6378` | Orbit ring on light |
| Violet | `#5B21B6` | Motto on light |

In the seller app, the logo uses the app's own Midnight colours (`#120A1F` with a Dusk ring) to sit on its light pages.

**Two name styles**
- **Futuristic:** Michroma, wide and letter-spaced (+22%). For posters, social media, presentations and the AQUENORA admin.
- **Bold:** Bricolage Grotesque Bold with its distinctive Q, letter-spaced (+8%). It matches the name in the seller app. The motto is in Bricolage Grotesque Medium.

Both fonts are from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License). In these files the letters are turned into shapes, so they look the same everywhere.

**Files** (each logo comes in both name styles: `-futuristic` and `-bold`)
- `aquenora-app-icon.svg` / `-1024.png`: App Store / Google Play icon (square; the stores round the corners themselves). Also `-512.png` and `-180.png`.
- `aquenora-mark-on-dark` / `-on-light`: the symbol alone, transparent background.
- `aquenora-logo-horizontal-{dark|light}-{futuristic|bold}`: symbol + name + motto in a row (website header, documents, email signature).
- `aquenora-logo-stacked-{dark|light}-{futuristic|bold}`: symbol above name + motto (social profile picture, posters, flyers).
- `aquenora-wordmark-{futuristic|bold}-{white|black}.svg`: the name alone.

Use the SVG files wherever possible: they stay sharp at any size.
